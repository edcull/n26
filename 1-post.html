The Carrion Psalm

House Cawdor

The Psalm come home with one prize and one grudge. The Banewaters are theirs, but the Pyroclave is in Delaque hands.

Mordecai has not forgotten the shadow that cut him down. He spends time haggling at the trading post for mesh armour and a bio-booster. He won't be caught so easily again.

Ezekiel Crows takes Vermin Pyre and a fist of credits to the flesh-menders, and Vermin comes back with a bionic leg in place of his ruined one, ready to walk among the faithful once more.

Elijah Emberchoir spends the cycle recovering from the psychoteric sleep. He fixes an Exterminator to his chainaxe to burn the witch Delaque.

Blessed Filth gets a mono-sight so his next shot doesn't miss, and Gutter Cantor works the Psalm's turf for coin. Deus drills Amen until the Stig-Shambler's gait is steady enough for him to fire the stubbers on the move, and young Ratsong learns to keep his nerve.

The Midnight Theatre

House Delaque

The Theatre lost the still, but at least they kept the Pyroclave away from the Cawdor. The promethium is flowing, and the whisper network makes sure every drop finds a buyer. Most of the troupe spend the cycle quietly working the turf, and the credits roll in.

Deuteronomy recruits at the trading post, bringing new faces into the show. Growltiger is a Ghost recruited cheap from the Heaviside hab-layer, and Wit is a whisper merchant, an outsider long friendly to House Delaque, who sells his secrets to the troupe.

Mistoffelees' connection to the Psychoteric Choir deepens and he learns to speak Terrible Truths, and a Psychoteric Wyrm now coils at his feet. Rumpleteaser adds an autogun to his personal arsenal, a reliable backup for when the plasma gun lets him down again. Demeter's long rifle gains a las-projector, and Jellylorum practises the art of lying low.
