Intro

Hollowpoint sits in a gutted manufactorum where the bullet-makers once worked, and some of them still do. At its heart the great Furnace still burns, melting down scrap brass for the casting benches. Cases are cast by hand and sold by the sack, and whoever holds the place decides who gets them.

The Midnight Theatre need it badly. The Rendermarch cost them dearly: Mungojerrie lies close to death, and Growltiger and Jellylorum are still nursing their wounds. With so few fighters left standing, the Theatre cannot afford to be outgunned, and Deuteronomy means to take the bullet works before the Psalm grow any stronger.

But the underhive keeps no secrets from The Carrion Psalm for long. Word of the Delaque's plan reaches Mordecai, and the Word Keeper leads his congregation into the ruins to meet them. Neither gang knows exactly where the other will come from. They will find each other in the dark.

Setup

At stake
Hollowpoint (Bullet Den) territory
Battlefield effect
Plentiful Ammo: once per round, each gang may make a Reload action as a Free action.
Challenger
The Midnight Theatre
Table
2' × 2' - Deployment zones 6" from each corner rather than 9"
Deployment
Chance Encounter: the winner of a roll-off chooses a corner. Their deployment zone is anywhere within 6" of that corner and the opposite corner; their opponent deploys within 6" of the other two corners. Starting crews are split as evenly as possible between each gang's two zones.
Objective
Object Lesson: in each End phase, gangs earn 1 VP for each enemy model that went Out of Action that round, +1 VP if it was a Leader, Champion or Brute, and +1 VP if it was taken Out of Action by a Fight, Coup de Grâce or Sabotage action. First to 10 VP wins.
Theatre side job
Crippling Strike: in any End phase, at least half of the opponent's models on the battlefield are Seriously Injured or Engaged.
Psalm side job
Spread Unrest: any Active non-Beast fighter performs the Graffiti (Double) action in the enemy deployment zone.
Crews
Strike Force: both gangs use Custom (5).
Theatre: 5 fighters plus Rumpus, 855 rating
Psalm: 5 fighters, 895 rating
